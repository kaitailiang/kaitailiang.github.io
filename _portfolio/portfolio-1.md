We are keen to deliver the world-leading ICT research. The CRYPTAI lab was found in 2021, and its vision is to pave the way to advancement of ICT. The lab, led by Dr. Liang, focuses on (1) applied cryptography, in particular, searchable encryption, updatable encryption, lattice-based encryption/signature (post-quantum cryptography); (2) theoretical machine/federated learning; and (3) blockchain (e.g., smart contract, oracles) development. 

The lab is mainly supported by the funding from the TU Delft, H2020, Horizon Europe and other external grants. And it has published excellent research works in the related A* conferences (e.g., USENIX, NDSS, Asiacrypt, CVPR) and top-tier international journals (e.g., IEEE TIFS, IEEE TDSC).
